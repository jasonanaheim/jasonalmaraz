Oversight Board

Helping people find their way through an appeals experience.

Role / Product ManagerCodazen / Staffed at MetaFocus / Delivery & accessibility

01 / The challenge

The public-facing website needed to help people navigate an appeals process. The work brought together business and privacy requirements, an external agency’s designs, and engineering implementation.

02 / My contribution

I clarified workflows, translated requirements into user stories and acceptance criteria, and coordinated with stakeholders, designers, and engineers to support delivery.

03 / A key decision

During QA, I identified accessibility barriers in essential controls. I advocated for targeted changes to improve usability while preserving the broader design direction, then worked with the team to incorporate those changes into delivery.

04 / What I learned

Accessibility expectations belong in the initial design discussions. Making those requirements explicit early gives design and engineering a shared standard and reduces late rework.
