webDiplomacy

A redesigned map and gameplay interface for the online strategy game used in Meta AI’s CICERO research.

Role / Product ManagerCodazen / Staffed at MetaFocus / Gameplay interface

The game

Diplomacy is a strategy game built around negotiation and trust. Seven players compete for control of the map, forming alliances and coordinating moves to get ahead.

The interface

The game, on the map.

Territories, units, and the current phase come together in a single view.

Map interactions

See the controls in action.

Short recordings of orders, unit selection, and territory highlights.

Alongside the board

Keep the conversation in view.

Game information and player conversations open beside the map.

Meta AI’s CICERO

Meta used webDiplomacy to evaluate CICERO, an AI agent that combines strategic reasoning with natural-language conversations to negotiate and cooperate with human players.

My role

At Codazen, I worked with the game’s owner, designers, and engineers on the map and gameplay interface redesign.
