webDiplomacy

A redesigned map and gameplay interface for the online strategy game used in Meta AI’s CICERO research.

Role / Product ManagerCodazen / Staffed at MetaFocus / Gameplay interface

The game

Diplomacy is a strategy game built around negotiation and trust. Seven players compete for control of the map, forming alliances and coordinating moves to get ahead.

My role

I was the product manager at Codazen helping revamp webDiplomacy with a more modern look and feel. I worked with the game’s owner, designers, and engineers to define the experience and what we needed to preserve: the game logic, authentication, and core controls.

The goal was to support Meta’s researchers while keeping the experience grounded in how people play Diplomacy. My focus was on modernizing the interface around the existing game and making sure the essential interactions continued to work.

An open-source project

The redesign was a collaboration between Codazen and Meta’s FAIR team within the open-source webDiplomacy project. The June 2022 announcement describes the new UI as AGPL-licensed, with the existing board remaining available alongside it. Read the project announcement ↗
