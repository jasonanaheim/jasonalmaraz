webDiplomacy

Redesigning a map and gameplay experience for the platform used in Meta AI’s CICERO research.

Role / Product ManagerCodazen / Staffed at MetaFocus / Interface & prioritization

01 / The context

Diplomacy is a strategy game built around negotiation, cooperation, and competing territorial goals. Meta used webDiplomacy to evaluate CICERO, an AI agent combining strategic reasoning and natural language. My work focused on the game interface and delivery priorities.

02 / The challenge

What initially looked like a visual redesign involved deeper integration work. The interface needed to connect to the existing game engine, maintain authentication, and preserve how players entered orders.

03 / My contribution

I learned the game’s rules, worked with the owner to clarify essential interactions, and partnered with engineering to investigate dependencies. I translated those findings into requirements, user stories, and delivery milestones.

04 / A key decision

I prioritized a low-fidelity, playable desktop MVP before mobile support and further visual polish. The first question to resolve was whether players could use the redesigned interface to carry out essential gameplay interactions with the existing engine.

05 / The outcome

The team shipped the redesigned interface after securing additional budget and revising delivery milestones. I learned to investigate functional dependencies early in a redesign and validate the core experience before expanding scope.
